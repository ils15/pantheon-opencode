The candidate tree-total median is below the inherited 30k-input hypothesis, but one of five runs
is above 30k and the arm ranges overlap. This pair therefore does not establish a causal tree-total
input reduction or a wall-time gain. The stable first-request proxy is the supported efficiency
claim. Monetary cost remains unavailable; cache pricing semantics were not measured. First-request
figures are supporting observations only until the interleaved protocol is rerun.
